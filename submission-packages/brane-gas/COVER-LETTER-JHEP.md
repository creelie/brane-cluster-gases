Dear Editor,

We submit the manuscript "Cancellation of all ultraviolet divergences of quantum gravity by a
scale-invariant gas of branes" by Deep Bhattacharjee and Ushashi Bhattacharya for publication in
the Journal of High Energy Physics.

The paper proves one theorem. A frozen Poisson gas of branes with no preferred size multiplies
the graviton kinetic operator by an entire function without zeros, exp[(n/2) Ein(Box^2/M^4)]
times a random factor with a limit at high momentum, and for n >= 4 the theory of gravity built
on that operator has no ultraviolet divergence at any order of perturbation theory: every graph
with two or more loops converges, the one-loop poles are those of a local theory and are
cancelled by three quartic operators with finite coefficients computed in closed form, Newton's
constant and the cosmological constant do not run, the zero-point energy is finite and regulator
independent, and the physical S matrix is unitary at every loop order. A one-loop graph in
closed form confirms the cutting rule and shows that the loop expansion is small only below an
energy between 1.4M and 1.9M. The paper states what it does not prove: the free Euclidean field
is a Gaussian measure that is not reflection positive, and no construction beyond perturbation
theory is claimed; the obstacles to one are named in the text.

Relative to the nonlocal-gravity literature, in which finiteness for entire form factors of
polynomial growth is known (Tomboulis, Modesto, Modesto-Rachwal), the new elements are that the
form factor is derived from a physical medium rather than chosen, that the theory is defined by
one realization of the gas with its random part controlled in closed form, that the class of
admissible single-brane responses is characterized and shown to be exactly the class without
zeros, that the fine-graining condition on the gas is forced by a regulator-independent vacuum
energy, and that the one-loop tuning, the vacuum energy, the closed-form bubble and its breakdown
energy, the regularity and the failure of reflection positivity of the free Euclidean field, and
the Newtonian well are computed for this class. The introduction states these points and the
discussion sets the construction beside the alternatives in a table.

Every result is derived in the text. The verification code and the independent audit scripts
that re-evaluate the formulas are public at https://github.com/creelie/brane-cluster-gases/releases/tag/v3.2.0 (release 3.2.0 of the
archive, which also holds the manuscript and its source) and are cited in the Data availability
section. The manuscript is deposited on Hexagon (hexagonmath.org) and is not on arXiv. It was
previously submitted to Physical Review D under an earlier title and was not accepted there; it
has been revised throughout since and is not under consideration elsewhere. The use of AI tools
is disclosed in the manuscript; the authors directed the work, checked every result and are
responsible for the content. The authors have no competing interests and received no funding.

# AI provenance statement

Manuscript: "Cancellation of all ultraviolet divergences of quantum gravity by a scale-invariant
gas of branes", Deep Bhattacharjee and Ushashi Bhattacharya.

Artificial-intelligence tools (Claude Code, Anthropic) assisted with drafting and editing the
text, with algebraic and numerical calculations, with the verification code and with the scripts
that draw the figures. No AI system is an author. Every result in the paper is derived
analytically in the text; no statement rests on a numerical computation or on an AI output. Deep
Bhattacharjee carried out all computations and checks, in C, Python, Julia and Lean 4, with the
assistance of Claude Code. The authors directed the work, re-derived and checked its results, and
take full responsibility for the content. The paper's Data availability and Use of AI tools
sections state the same.

The verification suite (195 Python, 84 C and 114 Julia checks, 17 Lean 4 theorems) and five
independent audit scripts (74 checks) that re-evaluate the formulas are public at
https://github.com/creelie/brane-cluster-gases and released as version 3.2.0,
https://github.com/creelie/brane-cluster-gases/releases/tag/v3.2.0. They are
not part of a Hexagon submission, which does not accept code.
